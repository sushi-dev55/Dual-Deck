# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Dual Deck welcomes people with different backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and points
of view. Everyone participating in the project should be able to do so without harassment,
intimidation, or discrimination.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Discuss the work and its impact rather than attacking the person proposing it.
- Accept constructive feedback and acknowledge mistakes.
- Respect privacy, boundaries, and requests to stop an unwanted interaction.
- Make room for contributors who are learning and ask questions in good faith.
- Keep technical disagreement focused on evidence, tradeoffs, and project goals.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, or sustained disruption
- Discriminatory language, slurs, or demeaning personal remarks
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Impersonation, deliberate misrepresentation, or malicious manipulation
- Encouraging others to engage in prohibited behavior
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, release channels,
and other spaces managed by the Dual Deck project. It also applies when someone is representing the
project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not post sensitive details publicly. Open a minimal
[code-of-conduct contact request](https://github.com/sushi-dev55/Dual-Deck/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml&title=%5BConduct%5D%3A+Private+contact+request)
without naming people or describing the incident; choose **Other** for the area and ask a
maintainer to arrange a private follow-up. For conduct on GitHub, the platform's block and
abuse-reporting tools are also available.

Maintainers will review reports in context, limit disclosure to people needed for the review, and
take action they consider appropriate. Possible actions include a private warning, removal of
content, temporary participation restrictions, or a permanent ban. Retaliation against someone
who reports a concern in good faith is not permitted.

Reports made in bad faith or intended to harass another participant may themselves violate this
policy. Nothing in this policy limits a person's right to contact relevant platform operators or
local authorities when necessary.
